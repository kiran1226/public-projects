## Metrics not used

- **Accuracy.** Over 99% of slice-hours are normal, so a detector that never alarms scores above 99%.
- **Point-wise precision, recall and F1.** They score every hour separately, so a long outage outweighs many short ones and flagging hour 30 earns as much as flagging hour 2.
- **AUROC.** It has no operating point, and with this imbalance it stays high while the pager is unusable.

## Watched metrics

| sessions | Visits per hour | Separates 'fewer visitors' from 'visitors not buying' |

| payment_success_rate | Payment successes / attempts | Isolates provider failure from everything upstream |
| discount_rate | Discount / gross revenue | Catches coupon errors, where orders rise and revenue looks healthy |
| gross_margin_rate | (Net revenue - cost) / net revenue | Catches price errors that raise volume |
| net_revenue | Gross revenue - discount | Used to size impact, not to detect: it lags and mixes every cause |
